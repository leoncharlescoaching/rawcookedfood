HOW THIS WORKS

Each food has an average cooked yield. The app converts between the uncooked and cooked weight, then calculates macros from the original raw or dry amount.

Water loss and absorption vary with cooking time, temperature and batch size, so the cooked weight is always an estimate.

For the most accurate meal prep: weigh the full batch before and after cooking and use your own batch yield.
